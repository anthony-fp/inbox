# Launch video music shortlist

Status: superseded by [launch-video.md](launch-video.md). The video uses a track Pete made in Suno instead of a stock library track.

## Conclusion

Use Epidemic Sound on a Business plan. Its stems let you build the 31 s drop and the 59.5 s return from one track. Audition Kouba, Ayoub and Sum Wave first.

Artlist Music Pro is the main alternative. It is cheaper by a third-party report, but its own pages returned a block, so none of its terms or tracks are verified here.

Nobody has checked a single track's stems or vocals on a live Epidemic page. Confirm both in the player before paying.

## Recommendation

**Library: Epidemic Sound Business.** Reasons:

- It covers a company posting social campaigns and digital ads.
- Stems are part of the product, so you can pull the kick and bass out at 31 s and bring them back at 59.5 s.
- Its deep house tracks list BPM, length and an Instrumental Version.

Before buying, check three things in the player:

- Business includes stems.
- Business covers YouTube. Epidemic's YouTube page was not read.
- The price. Business Plus starts at $100 a month on an annual plan. Business may be lower.

**Audition first:**

1. Last Summer (Instrumental Version), Kouba. It is the closest to minimal deep house and has no vocals.
2. Love on Time, Sum Wave. At 4:29 it leaves room to cut the 31 s drop and the finale.
3. Moments (Instrumental Version), Ayoub. It is a backup with a similar feel and no vocals.

**Fallback:** Envato Elements MoodMode, track 5. It costs from $16.50 a month and is cleared for YouTube claims. It has a loop and short versions, but no stems, so the 31 s strip-back must be cut by hand at bar lines.

At 124 BPM a bar is about 1.94 s. Cut at 31 s, 59.5 s and 81.5 s on the nearest bar line.
